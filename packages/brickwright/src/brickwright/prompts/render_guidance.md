Use `render` to look at the current build: it creates the build, renders a multi-view contact sheet (front, side, top,
and isometric views), and shows you the image in the next message. Reach for it once the build succeeds to visually
check it reads as the requested object—text checks confirm a build is valid, not that it looks right. To inspect a
detail the contact sheet hides, pass `azimuth` and `elevation` to render a single view from that camera angle instead.
